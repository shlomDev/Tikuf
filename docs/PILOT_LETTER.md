## English
**Subject: Free pilot proposal - automatic detection of boardings without a Rav-Kav tap, no personal identification**

Dear Sir or Madam, my name is Shlomo, an IT infrastructure engineer with a software and Android background, founding a company that builds data tools for public transport. I propose a short, well-defined pilot.

**Proposal.** An overhead camera counts boardings anonymously and compares them with validation events. A boarding with no tap within a short window is flagged as an event (time, door, line, trip). When it is ambiguous, the system reports "one of two did not tap" instead of guessing. Output: load and validation statistics by line and hour, and inspector deployment targeted at trips and hours with a high non-validation rate.

**Privacy by design.** No face recognition or biometrics, no stored images or video, processing on the device, output limited to counts and timestamps, no enforcement against an individual based on video. We will comply with Israeli privacy law and obtain legal review before any installation.

**What exists.** A working prototype with anonymous tracking, boarding and alighting counts and no-tap flagging, plus a ride simulation. Simulation results rest on assumptions. **Accuracy on a real bus has not been measured; that is the purpose of the pilot.**

**Pilot.** One line, 3 to 5 vehicles, 6 to 8 weeks, one operator, no cost. Success metrics agreed in advance (count accuracy against a manual count, agreement with inspector checks). Weekly and final reports, including what did not work.

**We ask for:** (1) aggregate validation data only (timestamp, vehicle, line, validator or door ID) or permission to mount an optical reader facing the validator; (2) permission to install a dedicated camera on pilot vehicles or use existing cameras in coordination with the operator; (3) a professional contact.

I would welcome a 30-minute meeting. Sincerely, Shlomo | [phone] | [email] | [company, in formation]
